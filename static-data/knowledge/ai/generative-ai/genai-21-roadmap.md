# Generative AI Roadmap

You've covered the foundations of generative AI — from language models to diffusion models, from prompt engineering to production deployment. This roadmap shows where to specialize based on your interests.

## What You've Learned

By completing this course, you understand:
- ✅ LLM fundamentals (architecture, tokenization, generation)
- ✅ Prompt engineering and in-context learning
- ✅ Fine-tuning (LoRA, QLoRA, instruction tuning)
- ✅ Alignment (RLHF, DPO, Constitutional AI)
- ✅ RAG and embeddings
- ✅ Agents and tool use
- ✅ Image generation (diffusion, GANs)
- ✅ Multimodal (CLIP, LLaVA, GPT-4V)
- ✅ Audio generation
- ✅ Evaluation and safety
- ✅ Production deployment (LLMOps)

## Specialization Tracks

### Track 1: LLM Engineering & NLP

**Focus**: Building applications with language models.

**Next courses to take:**
1. **LLM Engineering** — advanced fine-tuning, evaluation, deployment
2. **NLP** — text understanding, information extraction, translation

**Key skills to develop:**
- Advanced RAG techniques (graph RAG, agentic RAG)
- Prompt optimization and evaluation
- LLM application architecture
- Cost optimization and caching

**Career paths**: AI Engineer, LLM Application Developer, NLP Engineer

### Track 2: Image & Video Generation

**Focus**: Creating visual content with AI.

**Next courses to take:**
1. **Computer Vision** — understanding visual data
2. **Generative AI** (deep dive) — advanced diffusion, ControlNet

**Key skills to develop:**
- Fine-tuning Stable Diffusion (Dreambooth, LoRA)
- ControlNet and image editing
- Video generation (Sora, Runway)
- 3D generation (NeRF, Gaussian Splatting)

**Career paths**: AI Artist, Creative Technologist, Visual AI Engineer

### Track 3: AI Agents & Automation

**Focus**: Building autonomous AI systems.

**Next courses to take:**
1. **AI Agents** (dedicated course) — advanced agent architectures
2. **Reinforcement Learning** — training agents through interaction

**Key skills to develop:**
- Multi-agent systems
- Tool development and integration
- Memory and planning
- Safety and control for agents

**Career paths**: AI Agent Developer, Automation Engineer, AI Platform Engineer

### Track 4: AI Safety & Alignment

**Focus**: Ensuring AI systems are safe and beneficial.

**Key areas:**
- Alignment research (RLHF, DPO, Constitutional AI)
- Red-teaming and adversarial testing
- Interpretability and explainability
- AI governance and policy

**Career paths**: AI Safety Researcher, Responsible AI Lead, AI Policy Advisor

### Track 5: Multimodal & Foundation Models

**Focus**: Building and training large multimodal models.

**Key areas:**
- Vision-language models (CLIP, LLaVA)
- Audio-language models
- Video understanding
- Training from scratch

**Career paths**: Research Scientist, Foundation Model Engineer

## Advanced Topics to Explore

### Model Efficiency
- Quantization (GPTQ, AWQ, GGUF)
- Pruning and distillation
- Speculative decoding
- Edge deployment (MLC, llama.cpp)

### Advanced RAG
- Graph RAG (knowledge graphs + retrieval)
- Agentic RAG (LLM decides when/how to retrieve)
- Multi-modal RAG (images, tables, code)
- Self-RAG (model decides if retrieval is needed)

### AI Safety
- Interpretability (mechanistic interpretability)
- Alignment tax (safety vs. capability trade-offs)
- AI governance frameworks
- Responsible scaling policies

### Emerging Areas
- **AI for science**: Drug discovery, materials, climate
- **Embodied AI**: Robotics, autonomous vehicles
- **AI companions**: Long-term interaction, emotional intelligence
- **Code generation**: AI-assisted software development

## The Learning Mindset

1. **Build projects**: The best way to learn is by doing
2. **Read papers**: Follow arXiv, Papers With Code
3. **Join communities**: AI Twitter, Discord servers, local meetups
4. **Contribute to open source**: Hugging Face, LangChain, vLLM
5. **Stay curious**: The field evolves monthly

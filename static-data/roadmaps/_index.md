## How to Use These Roadmaps

1. **Pick your role** — or follow multiple if you're exploring
2. **Follow the sequence** — each step builds on the previous, don't skip ahead
3. **Click through to topics** — each step links to a deep-dive course
4. **Revisit as needed** — these are living documents
